The Aldol Reaction
Base-catalysed C–C bond formation
α-hydrogens are acidic (pKa ≈ 17) because the resulting anion is resonance-stabilised.
Step 1: Deprotonation of the α-carbon
CH3CH2CHO  +  −OH CH3CH−CHO  +  H2O
Resonance: the enolate
Enolate: negative charge shared between α-C and O. α-C is the nucleophilic site.
Step 2: Aldol addition
Alkoxide intermediate: the negative charge now sits on oxygen.
Step 3: Protonation
OH− regenerated → base is catalytic.
alkoxide  +  H2O β-hydroxy aldehyde  +  −OH
Product: the aldol
3-hydroxy-2-methylpentanal
a β-hydroxy aldehyde: the “aldol”

alcohol
